Living Blood — Lore

It was midnight, just like any other night. But someone was running in terror, desperately trying to escape.

Behind them, a zombie-like humanoid was chasing them.

Suddenly, the person tripped and fell to the ground. The humanoid caught up to them and bit them.

For some unknown reason, the humanoid suddenly died.

The bitten person grabbed their leg and continued running, desperately trying to escape. The humanoid had bitten them on the leg.

The next day, the man's eyes had turned red. He could no longer feel anything.

He tried to ask people for help, but he was unable to speak.

Eventually, the man died.

A large crowd gathered around his body to find out what had happened. The police identified him and contacted his family.

He was later buried.

Three hours after the body was buried, something strange happened inside the grave.

The corpse began to transform.

The person inside the coffin slowly turned into another zombie-like humanoid.

It broke through the wooden coffin, but soil filled its mouth, preventing it from breathing. It eventually died.

The next day, a guard inspecting the cemetery noticed that part of the soil above the grave had collapsed.

The guard was horrified by what he saw and immediately became frightened.

About an hour and a half later, chemists and police officers arrived at the scene.

Officer Maximus asked the chemists what had caused this strange incident.

One of them simply replied:

“We don't know.”

The next day, all the chemists who had examined the corpse died.

At first, they believed it was some kind of strange virus.

But they didn't know the truth.

It was Living Blood.

Living Blood was a mysterious type of blood that could enter any human blood group without rejection. It was also more powerful than the human mind itself.

Once it entered a victim's body, it would settle inside them and begin multiplying itself.

Within one day, Living Blood spreads throughout the victim's body and begins multiplying.

Eventually, it stops the victim's heart, killing them.

After the victim dies, the Living Blood moves into the brain and begins controlling the body.

It does this because it is searching for another host.

If Living Blood fails to find another host within four days, it will completely disappear, breaking down until not even a single molecule remains.

The next day, five more of the dead chemists successfully broke out of their graves.

They began searching for new hosts.

Living Blood was extremely intelligent. Whenever there were too many people nearby, it would observe them carefully and release a bloody vapor toward them.

Everyone became infected.

Commander Harry, who was in charge of the soldiers, ordered them to kill all the infected people.

Later, a sample was taken from this living organism. Only highly protected personnel were allowed to carry out the procedure.

The city was evacuated.

Before leaving, everyone was thoroughly examined for signs of infection. Anyone who attempted to leave illegally was killed.

Later, the Living Blood was frozen and stored at a temperature of −10°C.

The freezing temperature did not kill the Living Blood. Instead, it only put it into a dormant state, temporarily stopping its activity.
