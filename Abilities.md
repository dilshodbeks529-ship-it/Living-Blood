Living Blood — Abilities

1. Controlling

Living Blood can take control of an infected host's body. Once it reaches the brain, it can control the host and use the body to search for another host.

2. High Intelligence

Living Blood possesses extremely high intelligence. It can think independently, analyze its surroundings, make decisions, and control itself without relying on the host's mind.

3. Unusual Spread

Living Blood can spread through unusual methods of transmission. It can enter a host through different forms of exposure and adapt to different ways of spreading.

4. Blood Compatibility

Living Blood can enter and survive within any human blood group without being rejected by the body.

5. Self-Replication

Once inside a host, Living Blood can reproduce itself and spread throughout the host's body.

6. Host Control After Death

After killing its host by stopping the heart, Living Blood can move into the brain and take control of the body.

7. Dormancy

Living Blood can survive freezing temperatures. At −10°C, it does not die but enters a dormant state, temporarily stopping its activity.

8. Four-Day Survival Limit

If Living Blood cannot find another host within four days, it will completely disappear, breaking down until not even a single molecule remains.

9. Blood Vapor

When surrounded by a large number of people, Living Blood can release a bloody vapor to infect nearby individuals.

10. Body Consumption

Living Blood can consume parts of its host's body.

11. Extreme Survival Ability

Living Blood can survive inside a host, continue multiplying, and remain active even after the original host has died.
